Research Scope & Technical Domains

The 2nd International Conference on Machine Intelligence and Data Science (MIDAS-2027) provides a premier interdisciplinary forum for researchers, engineers, practitioners, and academicians to present original research, groundbreaking paradigms, and real-world industrial implementations across intelligence systems and data science.

Track 1: Intelligent Systems

Foundational algorithms, cognitive architectures, and intelligent computing paradigms:

Machine Learning Deep Learning Expert Systems Artificial Intelligence Neural Networks Cognitive Computing High Performance Computing

Track 2: Data Science Applications

Scalable analytics, database frameworks, security, and knowledge discovery:

Data Management & Quality Analysis Big Data Analysis Data Warehousing Data Security & Privacy Data Mining Data Visualizations Cloud Computing
Peer Review & Springer Proceedings

All submitted conference papers will be blind peer reviewed by three competent reviewers. It is planned to publish the proceedings with Springer in their Communications in Computer and Information Science series (final approval pending).
